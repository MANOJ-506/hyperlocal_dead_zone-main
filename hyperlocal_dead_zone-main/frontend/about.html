About the Locator

Understanding the mission to map, visualize, and report mobile coverage gaps.

Project Overview

The Hyperlocal Dead Zone Mobile Network Locator is a community-driven web platform designed to crowdsource reports of poor mobile network coverage. When users encounter connection issues, call drops, or slow speeds, they can pin their exact location to a public map.

By analyzing overlapping signal drop complaints, the system identifies persistent local coverage gaps and automatically compiles incident summaries for telecom providers, encouraging them to optimize cellular tower parameters in affected neighborhoods.

Problem Statement

Millions of mobile subscribers face daily connectivity problems such as call dropouts, weak signals indoors, or complete data connection blackouts. However, individual reports are rarely sufficient for telecom NOCs (Network Operations Centers) to allocate resources for optimization.

Existing reporting systems are manual, slow, and lack geographic context. This project solves this limitation by providing a live, public coverage map and automating email alerts when critical clusters of reports accumulate in a single area.

Objectives of V1 (MVP)

  • Provide users with a responsive, single-click interface to share GPS coordinates and specify their carrier.
  • Render a live interactive coverage map demonstrating signal gap markers color-coded by carrier.
  • Establish proximity algorithms that detect clusters of reports (e.g. 3 reports within a 200-meter radius).
  • Automate notifications containing detailed coordinate summaries and carrier information to support teams.
