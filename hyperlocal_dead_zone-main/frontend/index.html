How It Works

Reporting and tracking mobile network drops is simple and community-driven

1

Share Location

Allow GPS location permission so we can mark the exact coordinates of the connection drop.

2

Select Provider

Choose your mobile operator (Airtel, Jio, VI, or BSNL) and choose the issue type.

3

Submit Complaint

Add details of the issue (no signal, call drops, slow speed) and submit the form.

4

View Live Map

See the issue visualized on our map immediately. Overlapping reports trigger telecom notifications.
